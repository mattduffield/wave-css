wc-org-chart

Event org chart: leadership tiers, stations grouped by area, leads and members. Drag a member (mouse: anywhere on the row; touch or pen: the ⠿ grip) to another station or to Not placed, or use a member's ⋯ menu → Move to… (keyboard and phone path). Every move fires a cancelable wcorgchartmove; this demo "saves" it and fails on purpose when Fail next save is checked, calling chart.revert(memberId).
