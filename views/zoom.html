A status-bar zoom control: − / slider / + / percentage. The slider is logarithmic with 100% in the middle. Click the percentage for presets and Fit page / Fit width, or type a value. Over the board: Ctrl/⌘ + wheel or pinch zooms toward the cursor; Ctrl/⌘ + = / − / 0 / 1.
