wc-select chip mode — pick many in a row

Type, pick, type again: the list comes back as you type (or click the input, or press ↓) — no need to leave the field. A mouse pick keeps the cursor in the input. When the chips fill the row, the input moves to its own line so it (and its list) stay wide enough to use.
