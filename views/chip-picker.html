wc-chip-picker

Pick several values, each limited to some slots — parts × show nights, roles × shifts. One chip = one value + its slots; no slots = all. Click a chip (or + Add) to edit; ←/→ move between chips, Enter/Space opens, Delete/Backspace removes, Esc closes. With warn-overlap, chips sharing a slot turn amber (hover for who/when) — the choice is never blocked.

unique + short slot labels

      
A part may appear more than once, but never on overlapping nights. In the editor, nights a part already
        has are crossed out; pick a part that would overlap and a message names the clash and Done waits until you
        choose other nights. A part that already covers every night is disabled ("already added"); when every part covers
        every night, + Add part disappears.
        Chips use each night's short text ("Dec 6 (Sat)"); the last night has none, so it falls back to the first word of its label ("Sun").
